Know It settings

Hover over a YouTube thumbnail and click Know it to get the answer to its headline without opening the video.

Off by default. Gets answers ready for videos under 9 minutes that stay on your screen for a few seconds, so they open instantly. This sends those videos to Google even if you never click them, and uses your daily limit. It stops at 4 of the 8 hours, which leaves the rest for your own clicks.

Free daily limit

Gemini's free tier allows 8 hours of YouTube video a day, resetting at midnight Pacific time. Saved answers do not count.

Your data

When you ask for an answer, the video's link, title, channel, description and tags go to Google's Gemini API using your key. Nothing goes to the maker of this extension. On Gemini's free tier, Google may use what it receives to improve its products, and people may review it. See Gemini's terms, which require you to be 18 or older.

Know It is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google. Answers come from an AI model and can be wrong.
